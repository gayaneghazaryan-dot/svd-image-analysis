Subject: Submission of manuscript — “Singular Value Decomposition in Image Processing and Data Analysis: Theory, Algorithms, and Reproducible Experiments”

Dear Editor,

We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled
“Singular Value Decomposition in Image Processing and Data Analysis: Theory, Algorithms, and Reproducible Experiments”
for consideration in Mathematical Problems of Computer Science (MPCS).

This work presents a unified and reproducible framework for applying the Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) to three fundamental data analysis problems — image compression, image denoising, and principal component analysis (PCA) — with an adaptive rank-selection strategy based on cumulative energy and elbow detection.
The manuscript combines theoretical derivations with computational experiments, implemented in an open-source Python toolkit designed for full reproducibility.

The topic lies at the intersection of numerical linear algebra and applied data analysis, directly aligning with the scope of MPCS.
All numerical results, figures, and tables can be regenerated using the accompanying open repository:

https://github.com/gayaneghazaryan-dot/svd-image-analysis

Although this is our first submission on this particular topic, the work reflects extensive independent research and a commitment to reproducible and transparent scientific methodology.
We believe the paper may be of interest to the MPCS readership due to its blend of theoretical insight, algorithmic design, and practical implementation.

We confirm that the manuscript has not been published elsewhere and is not under consideration by another journal.
All authors have approved the final version and agree with its submission to Mathematical Problems of Computer Science.

We sincerely thank you for your time and consideration.
